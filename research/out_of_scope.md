# Out of scope

- Implementing or tuning the next World Model architecture.
- Running, installing, or benchmarking external repositories.
- Claiming that a lower one-step prediction loss implies better control.
- Treating repeated seeds on one deterministic CityFlow flow as independent
  statistical replications.
- Camera/video generation, visual traffic simulation, and pixel-based control.
- Real-world signal deployment, hardware integration, or sim-to-real claims.
- Copying code from repositories without an explicit compatible license.
- Treating every traffic predictor, model-based controller, or paper containing
  "model" as a complete World Model.
- Promoting raw notes or generated summaries to Obsidian.
- Calling the working hypothesis a novel contribution before L10-L12 pass.
